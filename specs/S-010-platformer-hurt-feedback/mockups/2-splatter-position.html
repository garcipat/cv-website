Where should the blood splatter anchor?

Same splatter style (C) picked in mockup 1, three anchor points compared. Option B (contact side) was picked — see mockup 3 for the final version.

A. Body center

Anchored at the sprite's torso/center, symmetric regardless of which side the hit came from. Simple, consistent, works identically for player and enemy.

B. Contact side

Offset toward whichever side the hit came from (here: the right, where the enemy/hazard touched). Reads as "hit from there" but needs a direction at the moment of damage.

C. Ground / feet level

Low, near ground contact — matches where most hits actually happen (side-touch, stomp) and keeps the face/readable silhouette clear.
